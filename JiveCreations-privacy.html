Privacy policy

JiveCreations Pin Poster

Effective September 26, 2026

JiveCreations operates this private tool solely for our own Pinterest business account, @jivecreations28. It publishes our original images and links to our Etsy listings on our own boards. It is not offered to other users.

Data we use

The app collects no end-user personal data. It uses a Pinterest access token to read our boards, publish our Pins, and verify our own posting history. We supply the images, captions, and listing links. The app does not collect Pinterest passwords or access other users' private data.

Local storage and control

The token is supplied through an environment variable and is never written to logs. The app keeps local post history and activity logs, including Pin IDs, timestamps, publishing details, and errors. These records support duplicate prevention and troubleshooting; the owner can delete them or revoke app access at any time. Deleting local records does not delete published Pins.

Sharing and this page

Publishing content is sent to Pinterest to perform the requested action. We do not sell personal data or use the app for advertising tracking. This page has no forms, analytics, or cookies; its hosting provider may process normal request information to serve it.
